After-School & Saturday Classes

For families whose children attend another school during the day, our after-school and Saturday language classes keep a second language growing — with English enhancement and cultural activities alongside. A great option for heritage families and first-time beginners alike. Classes are offered at our own campus and at public and private school sites.
