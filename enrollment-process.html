Enrollment

How to enroll

Enrolling at APLS is simple, and we're here to help at every step. We welcome new families year-round, so you can start the process any time.

Enrollment in three steps

  1. 1

    Schedule a tour

    Come visit, meet the teachers, and see a class in session. It's the best way to know if APLS is the right fit — no pressure.

    Schedule a tour
  2. 2

    Submit your application

    Complete the application form for your child's program and return it to the office. Find every form on the Application & Policy Forms page.

    View application forms
  3. 3

    Register & start

    Once your application is reviewed, we'll confirm your child's spot, complete registration, and welcome your family to APLS. We'll walk you through everything you need for the first day.

Questions about the process? Call our administrative office at 425-641-1703 — we're happy to help.
