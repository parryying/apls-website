Dual-Language Kindergarten & 1st Grade

Our accredited Kindergarten and 1st grade blend a full Common Core curriculum with second-language immersion, so children build reading, math, and social skills and real bilingual fluency at the same time — with at least 70% of the day in Chinese or Japanese.

Ages 5–6 Chinese/English or Japanese/English

Kindergarten

  • Accredited by the Washington State Board of Education
  • Native Chinese and Japanese teachers with US university degrees, state-certified
  • Common Core curriculum taught in the target language
  • At least 70% target-language immersion, with assistant help for non-native speakers
  • Low student-to-teacher ratio

Our goal

  • Build a strong foundation in all four language skills: speaking, listening, reading, and writing.
  • Deepen each child's understanding of Chinese or Japanese culture through language study.
  • Shape culturally sensitive, globally aware, responsible world citizens.

Our approach

  • Language basics — express needs and wants, speak in complete sentences, ask questions, enjoy rhymes, songs, and riddles, and practice new vocabulary in the target language.
  • Writing & reading — learn writing fundamentals, use basic words in sentences, apply writing concepts, build oral language and reading vocabulary, and respond to stories and texts with understanding.
  • Math — develop number sense, use numbers to describe the world, count in sets, and lay the groundwork for addition, subtraction, patterns, and algebra — all in Chinese or Japanese.

1st Grade

Ages 6–7 Chinese/English or Japanese/English

First grade continues the accredited, Common Core path — reading, writing, math, and science — woven together with growing fluency in Chinese or Japanese, taught by the same certified, native-speaking teachers your child already knows.

  • Complete, accredited first-grade curriculum
  • At least 70% target-language immersion
  • Low student-to-teacher ratio
  • Second-language literacy alongside English literacy
  • Develops all four skills: speaking, listening, reading, and writing
  • A continuous path onward from our Kindergarten
