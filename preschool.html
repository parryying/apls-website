Chinese & Japanese Immersion Preschool in Bellevue

APLS is an accredited Chinese immersion preschool and Japanese bilingual preschool in Bellevue, WA, immersing children ages 2½–6 in Mandarin Chinese or Japanese during the years a second language comes most naturally — no prior knowledge of the language required. Through language and culture, we spark curiosity and imagination while building the social, emotional, and cognitive skills that make children school-ready.

Ages 2½–6 Chinese or Japanese

What we offer

  • Full immersion in Chinese or Japanese
  • Full- and half-day programs, with before- and after-school care
  • Low child-to-teacher ratios in a safe, nurturing environment
  • Native-speaking teachers trained in early childhood education
  • Year-round enrollment, flexible scheduling, and an outdoor play area

What the day looks like

Our play-based curriculum follows school-readiness guidelines, helping children acquire a second language through literature, music, arts & crafts, and themed study. Three kinds of learning are woven through each day:

  • Free-choice activity time — children explore crafts, puzzles, drawing, and reading as teachers guide friendship and social skills.
  • Language art — characters, numbers, shapes, colors, a weekly theme, and culture, all in the target language.
  • Group learning time — a weekly theme explored through art, science, and music projects.

Chinese & Japanese immersion preschool — FAQ

Is APLS a Chinese immersion preschool in Bellevue?

Yes. APLS is an accredited Chinese immersion preschool in Bellevue, WA for children ages 2½–6. Families can also choose full Japanese immersion. Children learn Mandarin Chinese naturally through play, stories, music, and daily routines — no prior language knowledge is required.

Do you offer a Mandarin Chinese bilingual preschool program?

Yes. Our preschool is a bilingual immersion program: children are surrounded by Mandarin Chinese (or Japanese) throughout the day while building English-based school-readiness skills, so they grow comfortable in both languages.

What ages does the preschool serve?

Our Chinese and Japanese immersion preschool serves children ages 2½ to 6, with full- and half-day options plus before- and after-school care.

Does my child need to speak Chinese or Japanese already?

No. We welcome complete beginners. Young children acquire a second language naturally through immersion, so no prior Chinese or Japanese knowledge is needed.

Where is your Chinese immersion preschool located?

APLS is at 14042 NE 8th Street, 1st Floor, Bellevue, WA 98007, serving families across Bellevue and the Eastside including Redmond, Kirkland, Sammamish, and Issaquah.
