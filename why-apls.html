Since 1995 · Bellevue

A second language is a gift that lasts a lifetime. We've been giving it for 30 years.

Since 1995, Eastside families have trusted APLS to raise curious, confident bilingual children — in Mandarin Chinese and Japanese — inside a warm, accredited, non-profit school where every child is truly known.

Our story

In 1995, a small group of educators believed something that still guides us today: that any child — from any family, speaking any language at home — can become truly fluent in a second language, if they're given warmth, time, and teachers who stay. That belief grew, over three decades, into an accredited immersion school where Eastside families raise bilingual children in Mandarin Chinese and Japanese.

We were never trying to be the biggest or the most exclusive school. Our mission has always been quieter than that: to create a warm, continuous learning environment — from the earliest preschool years through the older grades — where children can explore language and culture with joy, at their own pace, guided by teachers who truly know them.

Thirty years on, that belief still guides everything we do. Many of the children who started with us as toddlers have grown into confident bilingual speakers — and some of our earliest families now send their younger children, and their friends, to APLS.

Why learn a second language early?

The early years are a once-in-a-lifetime window. Young children absorb language naturally — and the benefits last far beyond the classroom.

🪟

A window of opportunity

Young children have a natural ability to learn language. They can pick up multiple languages with near native-like proficiency — while adults often struggle to understand and pronounce new sounds.

🧠

Cognitive & academic benefits

Studies link learning a second language to stronger communication skills, higher standardized test scores, better problem solving, and better memory.

🌏

Lifelong benefits

A second language helps children understand other cultures, learn additional languages more easily, and gain an advantage in the global marketplace.

A community, not just a school

Language lives inside culture — so it's woven through everything we do. Across the year, our children celebrate Lunar New Year, Japanese seasonal festivals, storytelling, music, arts, and food, alongside families and friends. These moments aren't extras; they're how young children come to love a language and feel proud of where it comes from.

Many of our events are open to the wider community. We see this as part of our purpose: sharing the joy of language and culture, and welcoming every family who's curious — whatever their background.
