Frequently asked questions

Does my child need to know Chinese or Japanese already?

No. Our programs welcome complete beginners — children acquire the language naturally through immersion, no prior knowledge required.

Do you offer both Chinese and Japanese?

Yes. APLS teaches both Mandarin Chinese and Japanese across our programs.

What ages do you serve?

From age 2½ in preschool through high-school age for AP prep, depending on the program. See Programs for details.

Is APLS accredited and licensed?

Yes. APLS is an accredited private school licensed by the Washington State Office of Superintendent of Public Instruction, and our learning center is a licensed childcare center. We are an independent non-profit.

When can we enroll?

We offer year-round, rolling enrollment. Reach out any time and we'll help you find a start date that works.

What are your office hours?

Our office is open Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m. Individual program hours vary.
