Tuition

As an independent non-profit, APLS is committed to keeping high-quality bilingual education within reach for Eastside families. Here's what tuition looks like across our programs.

The rates below are for our Preschool (Learning Center) program. For Kindergarten, 1st grade, after-school, Saturday classes, summer camp, and private lessons, please contact us for current pricing.

Preschool monthly tuition

Choose a half-day morning session (9:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m.) or a whole-day program (8:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.), 2 to 5 days per week.

Registration & other fees

  • Registration fee & deposit — $175 per child. A $75 non-refundable registration fee plus a $100 deposit that holds your child's spot and is applied toward the first month's tuition. Due when the Enrollment Application Form is signed. The deposit is refundable if enrollment is cancelled.
  • Annual material fee — $75 half-day / $100 whole-day. Covers textbooks and art supplies; the whole-day program also includes sleeping sheets.
  • Extra care — $15 per hour (one-hour minimum) for registered students.
  • Overtime — $35 per 30-minute interval if a child is not picked up by the scheduled time. This fee is billed with the next scheduled payment.
  • Non-potty-trained children. An additional fee applies, as we provide teaching assistants for toileting help. Parents supply pull-ups (no diapers), baby wipes, and plastic bags.
  • Sibling discount — 10% for siblings of enrolled students.

Our commitment to affordability

We keep our rates as accessible as we can so that more children can experience the lasting gift of a second language. If cost is a concern, please talk to us — we're always glad to discuss options and find a way forward together.
