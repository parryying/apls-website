Why families choose APLS

A warm, accredited language journey with experienced teachers and a clear path from preschool onward.

🧑‍🏫

Small classes, dedicated teachers

Small classes average a 1:6 teacher-to-student ratio, with college-educated teachers and low turnover.

💛

Warm, kind & joyful

Play, music, art, and cultural celebration help children feel safe, known, and excited to learn.

📚

Real, lasting fluency

An accredited immersion path builds speaking, reading, and writing skills from preschool onward.

🌱

Non-profit & affordable

As a non-profit school, APLS directs tuition toward teachers, classrooms, and children.
