Preschool Immersion

Ages 2½–6 Chinese or Japanese

Our youngest learners spend their day fully immersed in Mandarin Chinese or Japanese — the ages when a second language comes most naturally. Days are filled with music, stories, arts & crafts, and themed play, all in the target language, guided by warm teachers who make each child feel at home.

  • Full immersion in Chinese or Japanese — you choose the path
  • Learning through song, story, art, and play — never flashcards and pressure
  • A gentle first-school experience for children with no prior language exposure

Dual-Language Kindergarten

Ages 5–6 Chinese/English or Japanese/English

Our accredited Kindergarten blends a full academic curriculum with a second language, so children build reading, math, and social skills and real bilingual fluency at the same time. It's a seamless next step for our preschoolers — and a welcoming entry point for families new to APLS.

  • Accredited academics taught alongside Chinese or Japanese
  • Small classes where every child is known by name
  • A continuous path onward into 1st grade and beyond

Dual-Language 1st Grade

Ages 6–7 Chinese/English or Japanese/English

First grade continues the bilingual journey with a full, accredited curriculum — reading, writing, math, science — woven together with growing fluency in Chinese or Japanese. Children who started with us as toddlers arrive here already confident; new families are warmly supported to catch up.

  • Complete, accredited first-grade curriculum
  • Second-language literacy alongside English literacy
  • The same caring teachers your child already knows

After-School & Saturday Classes

Ages 5–11 Chinese or Japanese

For families whose children attend another school during the day, our after-school and Saturday language classes keep a second language growing. They're a great option for heritage families who want to strengthen home language, and for beginners discovering Chinese or Japanese for the first time.

  • Flexible classes that fit around the regular school week
  • Grouped by age and level so every child is appropriately challenged
  • Chinese and Japanese tracks available
Schedule a tour

Summer Language & Culture Camp

Ages 2½–4th grade Chinese or Japanese

Summer at APLS is language and culture wrapped in pure fun. Our Sunflower camp welcomes the youngest children (2½–5), while our Regular camp serves K–4th graders — both filled with crafts, games, cooking, music, and stories in Mandarin or Japanese. It's the perfect way to try immersion, or to keep skills sharp over the break.

  • Sunflower Camp — ages 2½–5
  • Regular Camp — Kindergarten through 4th grade
  • A relaxed, joyful way for new families to experience APLS
Ask about summer camp

AP Prep & High School Credit

Ages 12–18 Chinese or Japanese

For older students, APLS offers focused preparation for AP Chinese and AP Japanese, plus tutoring toward high school language credit. Taught by experienced instructors, these classes help serious learners reach — and demonstrate — advanced proficiency.

  • AP Chinese and AP Japanese exam preparation
  • Support toward high school world-language credit
  • Experienced instructors who know the exams
