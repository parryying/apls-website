Preschool Immersion

Ages 2½–6 Chinese or Japanese

APLS Preschool immerses children in Mandarin Chinese or Japanese during the years a second language comes most naturally — no prior knowledge of the language required. Through language and culture, we spark curiosity and imagination while building the social, emotional, and cognitive skills that make children school-ready.

  • Full immersion in Chinese or Japanese
  • Full- and half-day programs, with before- and after-school care
  • Low child-to-teacher ratios in a safe, nurturing environment
  • Native-speaking teachers trained in early childhood education
  • Year-round enrollment, flexible scheduling, and an outdoor play area

What the day looks like

Our play-based curriculum follows school-readiness guidelines, with three kinds of learning woven through each day:

  • Free-choice activity time — children explore crafts, puzzles, drawing, and reading as teachers guide friendship and social skills.
  • Language art — characters, numbers, shapes, colors, a weekly theme, and culture, all in the target language.
  • Group learning time — a weekly theme explored through art, science, and music projects.

Dual-Language Kindergarten

Ages 5–6 Chinese/English or Japanese/English

Our accredited Kindergarten blends a full Common Core curriculum with second-language immersion, so children build reading, math, and social skills and real bilingual fluency at the same time — with at least 70% of the day in Chinese or Japanese.

  • Accredited by the Washington State Board of Education
  • Native Chinese and Japanese teachers with US university degrees, state-certified
  • Common Core curriculum taught in the target language
  • At least 70% target-language immersion, with assistant help for non-native speakers
  • Low student-to-teacher ratio

Our goal

  • Build a strong foundation in all four language skills: speaking, listening, reading, and writing.
  • Deepen each child's understanding of Chinese or Japanese culture through language study.
  • Shape culturally sensitive, globally aware, responsible world citizens.

Our approach

  • Language basics — express needs and wants, speak in complete sentences, ask questions, and enjoy rhymes, songs, and riddles in the target language.
  • Writing & reading — learn the basics of writing, build meaningful vocabulary, and demonstrate comprehension of text of all kinds.
  • Math — develop number sense, count in sets, and lay the groundwork for addition, subtraction, patterns, and algebra — all in Chinese or Japanese.

After-School & Saturday Classes

Ages 5–11 Chinese or Japanese

Summer Language & Culture Camp

Ages 2½–4th grade Chinese or Japanese

Summer at APLS is language and culture wrapped in pure fun — a great way to learn Mandarin Chinese or Japanese and make friends. Our Sunflower camp welcomes the youngest children, while Regular camp serves K–4th graders.

  • Sunflower Camp — ages 2½–5
  • Regular Camp — Kindergarten through 4th grade
  • Weekly registration with extended care available
  • Asian geography, stories & legends, abacus counting, arts & crafts, music & festivals, calligraphy, Asian food, hands-on projects, and park field trips
